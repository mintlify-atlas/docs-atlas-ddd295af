Airbyte Cloud is a fully managed version of Airbyte hosted by Airbyte, Inc. You get all the power of the Airbyte platform—600+ connectors, flexible sync modes, CDC support—without running or maintaining any infrastructure.

## Benefits

- **No infrastructure to operate.** Airbyte manages Kubernetes, upgrades, scaling, and reliability.
- **Automatic upgrades.** You always run the latest version of Airbyte with no manual upgrade steps.
- **Data residency options.** Choose which cloud region processes and temporarily stores your data.
- **Consumption-based pricing.** Pay for the data you move, not for idle infrastructure.
- **Enterprise SLAs.** Guaranteed uptime and support response times for qualifying plans.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Go to [cloud.airbyte.com](https://cloud.airbyte.com) and sign up for a free account. You can sign up with Google, GitHub, or an email address.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your workspace">
    After signing in, Airbyte creates a default workspace for you. A workspace is an isolated environment that contains your sources, destinations, and connections.

    You can rename your workspace or create additional workspaces from the workspace selector in the top navigation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a source">
    Click **Sources** in the left navigation, then click **New source**. Search for the connector you need, fill in the required credentials, and click **Set up source**.

    Airbyte Cloud supports the full catalog of 600+ connectors. Credentials are encrypted at rest.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a destination">
    Click **Destinations** in the left navigation, then click **New destination**. Select your destination connector, provide the connection details, and click **Set up destination**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a connection and sync">
    Click **Connections**, then **New connection**. Select your source and destination, configure streams and sync modes, set a schedule, and click **Set up connection**.

    Click **Sync now** to run your first sync immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data residency

Airbyte Cloud lets you choose the cloud region where your data is processed. This helps you meet data sovereignty and compliance requirements.

You can configure data residency at the workspace level. Available regions are listed in your workspace settings under **Data residency**.

<Info>
  Data residency controls where data is temporarily staged during a sync. Source and destination credentials are always stored encrypted in Airbyte's secure secrets manager.
</Info>

## Workspace and team management

- **Multiple workspaces:** Organize your connections by team, environment (dev/staging/prod), or business unit.
- **Role-based access control:** Invite team members and assign roles (Admin, Editor, Viewer) at the workspace level.
- **SSO:** Enterprise plans support single sign-on via OIDC-compatible identity providers.

## Cloud vs self-hosted

| | Airbyte Cloud | Self-Hosted |
|---|---|---|
| Infrastructure | Managed by Airbyte | Managed by you |
| Upgrades | Automatic | Manual |
| Data residency | Configurable by region | Your environment |
| Custom connectors | Yes | Yes |
| SSO | Enterprise plans | Enterprise license |

If you need to keep data entirely within your own environment, see the [self-hosted deployment options](/deploy/overview).
