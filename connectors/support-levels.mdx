Every connector in the Airbyte catalog belongs to one of four support levels. The level determines who maintains the connector, whether it has support SLAs, and who can use it.

## Support level summary

| | Airbyte | Enterprise | Marketplace | Custom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Maintained by** | Airbyte | Airbyte | Community | You |
| **Production readiness** | Guaranteed | Guaranteed | Not guaranteed | Not guaranteed |
| **Support: Cloud** | Supported\* | Supported\* | No support | Supported\*\* |
| **Support: Powered by Airbyte** | Supported\* | Supported\* | No support | Supported\*\* |
| **Support: Self-Managed Enterprise** | Supported\* | Supported\* | No support | Supported\*\* |
| **Support: Core** | Slack only | N/A | No support | Slack only |
| **Who can use them** | Everyone | Extra license required | Everyone | You only |

\* Official Support SLAs are available only to customers with Premium Support included in their contract.

\*\* For Custom connectors, Official Support SLAs require Premium Support. Support is provided on a best-effort basis; maintenance and upgrades are owned by the customer.

## Airbyte connectors

Airbyte connectors are built and maintained by the Airbyte team to a high quality standard.

- Tested, vetted, and production ready.
- Officially supported and available to all users.
- Experience few breaking changes. When an upgrade is required, you receive an adequate upgrade window.

These are the connectors you will find documented throughout this section, such as Postgres, MySQL, Salesforce, Snowflake, and BigQuery.

## Enterprise connectors

Enterprise connectors are premium connectors available exclusively for Self-Managed Enterprise and Pro customers **at an additional cost**.

- Built and maintained by the Airbyte team.
- Provide enhanced capabilities and support for critical enterprise systems.
- Not available to Open Source or standard Cloud customers.
- Support larger datasets, offer parallelism for faster data transfers, and are covered under Airbyte's support SLAs.

<Info>
Contact your Airbyte account representative to learn which Enterprise connectors are available and how to enable them.
</Info>

## Marketplace connectors

Marketplace connectors are maintained by Airbyte's community members.

- Not maintained by Airbyte.
- No support SLAs.
- Should be used with caution in production environments.
- May not be feature complete and can experience backward-incompatible breaking changes without notice.
- Available to everyone.
- Can be improved by community contributors.

<Warning>
Marketplace connectors do not carry production-readiness guarantees. Review the connector's documentation and success rate data before relying on it for critical pipelines.
</Warning>

## Custom connectors

Custom connectors are connectors you build and maintain yourself, available only within your workspace.

- You alone are responsible for their quality and production readiness.
- Official Support SLAs are only available if you have Premium Support included in your contract.
- Support is provided on a best-effort basis; maintenance and upgrades are your responsibility.

To build a custom connector, use the [Connector Builder](https://docs.airbyte.com/connector-development/connector-builder-ui/overview) for no-code HTTP API connectors, or the [Airbyte CDK](https://docs.airbyte.com/connector-development/cdk-python/) for more complex use cases.

## Archived connectors

From time to time, Airbyte removes a connector from the catalog. This typically occurs due to low usage or lack of community maintenance, and is necessary to ensure the catalog maintains a minimum level of quality.

- Archived connectors receive no further updates or support from Airbyte.
- They remain source-available in the [`airbytehq/connector-archive`](https://github.com/airbytehq/connector-archive) repository on GitHub.

To take over maintenance of an archived connector, open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/airbytehq/airbyte/discussions/). Connectors must pass [Acceptance Tests](https://docs.airbyte.com/platform/connector-development/testing-connectors/connector-acceptance-tests-reference) before the un-archiving process can begin.
